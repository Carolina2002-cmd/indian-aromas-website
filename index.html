Let's cook up a collaboration

INDIAN AROMAS welcomes collaborations with food creators, home bakers and brands that suit a vegetarian Indian kitchen. Here are a few ideas to start with.

  • Cook-along with a creator

    Two kitchens, one recipe. A split-screen video or a recipe swap between our channels.

  • Festival special series

    Holi, Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali and Christmas menus, planned weeks ahead of the festival.

  • Product & ingredient features

    Kitchen appliances, baking tools, spices and dairy, used honestly inside a real recipe.

  • Cake & decorating showcases

    Themed cakes for launches, celebrations and campaigns, from matki cakes to painting cakes.

  • Recipe challenges

    Viewer challenges, regional recipe swaps and festival cook-offs that get people cooking along.
